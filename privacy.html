Privacy

Steroid Pathway Trainer. Last updated November 12, 2026.

Steroid Pathway Trainer is a free study game for adrenal steroid pathways and congenital adrenal hyperplasia. It is a study aid, not clinical guidance. This page explains what the site stores and why.

If you play as a guest

Nothing leaves your device. Your scores, streak and weak spots are saved in your own browser's storage and stay there until you clear it.

If you create an account

An account exists for one purpose: to save your progress so you can pick it up on another device. The site stores:

  • Your email address, used to sign you in.
  • If you sign in with Google, the basic profile details Google shares at sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture. The site asks Google for nothing else and cannot see your Google password, mail, files or contacts.
  • Your game progress: scores, the days you played, and which items you tend to miss.

If you join the research study

Research mode is off unless you enter a study code given to you by a study coordinator and agree on the consent screen. While it is on, the page records how you use the trainer, linked only to that code: which items you answer, whether each answer is right, how long it takes, which named steroid or enzyme you gave when an answer is wrong, which mode you play, and when the page is open or hidden. It does not record your name, your email address, your account, or any text you type that the game does not recognize. These records are kept in a separate, locked area of the same Supabase project; browsers, signed in or not, cannot read them, and only the study investigators can. They are used only for the study described on the consent screen. "Leave study" stops recording on that device; to remove what was already recorded, contact your study coordinator.

How the information is used

Only to sign you in and to load and save your progress. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train anything. The site has no ads and no analytics or tracking scripts. The only usage records are those described above for study participants who opt in.

Where it is kept

Account details and progress are stored with Supabase, a database hosting service, on servers in the United States. Each account can read and change only its own progress record. The site's files are served by GitHub Pages, and one code library is loaded from the jsDelivr content network. Like any web host, those services may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests a file.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and progress to be deleted at any time by opening a request at the project's issue page. Guest progress is deleted by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change with it.
